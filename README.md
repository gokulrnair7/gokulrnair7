### Projects

**[Budget vs Actual Variance Analysis](https://github.com/gokulrnair7/sankalp-industries-budget-vs-actual-variance-analysis)**  
Python, SQL Server, Excel, Power BI

Opex variance across 36 months, 13 departments and Rs 3,758 Cr of budget. The headline said 4% over plan. Absolute variance was 8.7%, because underspends were cancelling out overspends before anyone saw the total. Also separates one-off events from recurring trends, which matters because the two need different responses.

**[Revenue and Profitability Analysis](https://github.com/gokulrnair7/vastra-junction-revenue-profitability)**  
SQL Server, Excel, Power BI

Retail apparel chain, 9,600 order lines. Gross margin falls from 45% at full price to 9.5% once discounts pass 30%. Includes a seasonal revenue forecast and a discount scenario model.

**[Integrated 3-Statement Model](https://github.com/gokulrnair7/integrated-3-statement-financial-model-mphasis)**  
Excel

Linked income statement, balance sheet and cash flow for Mphasis Ltd, extended to a three year driver based forecast with a Base, Bull and Bear scenario toggle.
